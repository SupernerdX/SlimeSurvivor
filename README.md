# Slime Survivor

**This project is currently in active development and is being used to explore gameplay systems, player progression, and moment-to-moment combat feel in Unity.**

Slime Survivor is a Unity gameplay prototype where the player controls a gelatinous cube that grows by consuming enemies, objects, and parts of the dungeon itself.

The project focuses on movement-driven combat, absorption, growth, and progression systems.

**Core Gameplay**

Rather than relying on traditional weapons, movement serves as both the player’s attack and defense.

As the slime consumes the environment and enemies, it grows larger and becomes capable of taking on stronger threats.

**Core Systems**

Current systems include:

Grid-based movement
Code-driven squash-and-stretch animation
Suspended items inside the slime simulated with spring dampers
Progression and growth systems

A major goal of this project was to challenge myself to create the character’s animation entirely through code rather than relying on traditional animation clips. The slime’s squash, stretch, and internal item motion are driven through vertex displacement and spring-damper systems, with exposed Inspector controls for tuning the behavior during development.
